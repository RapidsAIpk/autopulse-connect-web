App overview

How AutoPulse Connect works

An AI chat on the dealer's website that answers visitors from the dealer's own stock, hands the conversation to the team when needed, and turns it into a lead and a booked showroom visit. Everything lands in one inbox.

1 The flow: from website visitor to booked visit

One visitor, start to finish. Each step shows where it appears in the app.

  1. The chat bubble sits on the dealer's website (one line of embed code from Website chat). Pop-up messages next to it invite visitors to ask.
  2. The visitor picks a department (Sales, Service, Finance…) and gives their name, e-mail and phone. A returning visitor continues their earlier chat.
  3. The AI answers using the store's own stock, address, hours and links (Store → What the AI knows), and sends car cards with photo, price and a link to the car's page.
  4. The chat shows up live in the Inbox. Anyone on the team can open it, switch the AI off for that chat and reply. The visitor sees the reply in the same window.
  5. A lead is saved when the visitor fills the lead form, or the team clicks Save lead. A visit is booked from the chat or by the visitor. They appear under Leads and Appointments.
  6. The dealer is told: each lead and appointment is e-mailed to the dealer's CRM in ADF format, and the team gets alerts (chat waiting, person asked for, new lead).
  7. Everything is counted: Dashboard, Conversations, Visitors (pages and cars each visitor viewed) and Vehicles (leads, chats, views and taps per car).

2 Getting started (a new dealership)

The Dashboard's Get started list follows the same order and ticks each step off.

1

Store details

Store: address (Find on map), phones, contact person, manager alerts, and under What the AI knows the inventory, credit-application and trade-in links.

2

Website chat

Website chat: bot name, colours, welcome text, opening hours, pop-up messages. Copy the embed code to the website, then Open test page to try it like a customer.

3

Cars

Vehicles: stores in the AutoPulse feed get their stock automatically. Others use My own inventory: add cars (VIN fills the details), photos, CSV import.

4

Team

Team: add agents and managers with their departments; choose how new website chats are shared out (next person online, or the department queue).

5

E-mails

Settings → E-mails & alerts: who gets team alerts and where ADF lead e-mails go (the dealer's CRM).

6

Pages to share

Pages: a dealership page and one per person, with a QR code. Chats that start from a person's page go straight to them.

3 Every screen and what it is for

Grouped the way you use them. Each screen has Help (top of the menu) with a short tour.

Daily work

Dashboard

The store at a glance: cars, car-page views, leads, appointments, chats; Get started; trends over 7/30/90 days.

Inbox

Every conversation, newest first. Filter by status (Unread, Unreplied, Human, Queue…). Open a chat to reply, switch the AI off, flag, Save lead, Book visit, send cars from the inventory, attach files or a voice note.

Leads

Customers interested in a car, by customer or by car, with filters, details, open chat, delete and CSV export.

Appointments

Showroom visits booked from chats and the website, with counts, filters and CSV exports.

Vehicles

The stock with search filters and, per car, leads, chat mentions, page views and taps. Copy a car's public link.

Conversations

A log of every chat: first question, messages, session time, clicks, bookings. Export as CSV or transcripts.

Visitors

Who browsed the website (when tracking is on): identified, unverified or anonymous; pages and cars each one viewed; who came back.

Setting up the store

Website chat

The chat's look, texts, hours and app buttons; pop-up messages; visitor tracking; the embed code and test page.

Store

Address and map, contacts, call and text numbers, manager alerts, what the AI knows, and your stores.

Team

The dealership's people, their roles and departments, and how new chats are routed. Shows what each role may do.

Pages

The dealership page and each person's page with QR codes, to share on stickers, cards and e-mail signatures.

Settings

Your account (profile, time zone, template sign-off, password), AI switches, e-mails & alerts. Facebook & Instagram: Coming soon.

AutoPulse admins

Stores

Every dealership on AutoPulse with contact, subscription and leads; add, edit, pause or delete a store; store controls.

Inbox users

All sign-ins: create accounts, reset passwords, disable or delete.

Activity

Audit log (who did what), system status, website demo/contact enquiries, and calls to outside services.

Store switcher

Viewing at the top: pick the dealership to look at. Every screen shows only that store's data.

Delete all data

Removes everything about one customer (chats, leads, visits, visitor data) after showing what will go.

What customers see

Chat widget

Bubble and pop-ups, department choice, contact form, AI answers with car cards, live team replies, lead form and visit booking.

Car pages

A public page per car with photos, price, details and Call / Text / Chat buttons (taps are counted).

Dealership & staff pages

Address, hours, team, cars and QR code; "Chat with me" on a person's page.

4 Who can do what

RoleWhoCan
AutoPulse adminAutoPulse staffEverything, for every store: Stores, Inbox users, Activity, store controls, AutoPulse site settings, Delete all data.
OwnerThe dealership's account holderTheir own store(s): inbox, leads, visits, vehicles, visitors, website chat, store details, team, pages, e-mails.
ManagerAdded by the ownerThe inbox and the store's settings; the exact rights are in Team → What each role can do.
AgentAdded by the owner or a managerThe inbox for their departments: reply, save leads, book visits.

The server checks every action against the role, and each store only ever sees its own chats, leads and settings.

6 For testers

Where to test

Only in AutoPulse Test Dealership (pick it under Viewing). Real dealers then never get test chats or e-mails. Dealer e-mail is in test mode: lead and alert e-mails go to the AutoPulse test inbox.

The website chat

Website chat → Open test page opens the store's chat like a customer sees it. Use a name with letters only and a real-looking e-mail and phone (the form checks them).

Recording results

The Feature & Test Sheet (AutoPulse admins: Help → Feature & test sheet) lists every feature and button with steps and the expected result. Record Pass, Fail or Blocked with a note and screenshots.

Cleaning up

After a test, an AutoPulse admin can remove the test customer with Delete all data in the chat. Never use a real customer's details.

7 Words we use

Lead
A customer interested in a car, with their contact details, saved from a chat or a form.
ADF
The e-mail format dealer CRMs import leads from. Each new lead and appointment is sent in it.
Department
Sales, Service, Finance… The visitor picks one; it decides who gets the chat.
Human / take over
The team replies instead of the AI; the AI stays paused for that chat until switched back on.
Queue
A chat waiting for someone in its department.
Unreplied
The customer wrote last and nobody has answered yet.
Identified visitor
A website visitor who gave their details in the chat.
Test dealership
A store only for testing; its chats and e-mails never reach a real dealer.
Coming soon
A feature that is built but not switched on in this app yet (WhatsApp, SMS, Messenger, Instagram).
