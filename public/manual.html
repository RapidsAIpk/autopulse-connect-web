User manual

AutoPulse Connect, step by step

AutoPulse Connect puts an AI chat on your dealership website, answers customers from your live stock, and brings every conversation, lead and booked visit into one inbox your team works from.

← Back to the appFor dealership owners, managers and sales staffApp AutoPulse Connect (this website)Edition November 2026

01How it works

Every customer conversation follows the same path. The AI does the first part, and your team takes over whenever it wants to.

Visitoropens the chat bubble on your website and picks a department.
Detailsgives a name, e-mail and phone once, so you can follow up.
AI answersfrom your live stock, with car cards, prices and links.
Your teamsees the chat live, replies or takes over at any moment.
Lead or visitis saved, e-mailed to your CRM and listed in the app.

The screenshots in this manual come from a demo dealership with sample customers.

02Signing in

  1. Open the AutoPulse Connect sign-in page (Sign in on this website) and enter your e-mail and password.
  2. Dealership owners also get a 6-digit sign-in code by e-mail. Type it in and press Verify and sign in.
  3. New team members sign in with the temporary password they were given, then choose their own password (at least 12 characters).
Security. After five wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes. Changing your password signs out your other devices.

03Getting around

  • Menu (left): every screen of the app. Collapse menu at the bottom narrows it to icons.
  • Viewing (top): if you have more than one store, pick the one you are working on. Every screen follows it.
  • Live (top): shows that new chats and messages arrive without reloading the page.
  • Help: explains the screen you are on. Show me starts a short guided tour. The small ? icons explain each panel.
  • Theme: switches between light and dark. The app remembers your choice.

04Dashboard

Menu → Dashboard

Your store at a glance. Get started lists the setup steps still to do; each Set up button opens the right page. The quick actions and the cards (Vehicles, Car page views, Leads, Appointments, Chats) each open their screen.

Trends further down shows charts of chats, messages, first-reply times, busy hours, leads, visits and car interest. Pick 7 days, 30 days or 90 days, hover for numbers, or press Table to read them as a table.

05Inbox

Menu → Inbox

Every customer conversation, newest first. Click a chat on the left to open it on the right.

  • Platform tabs: All platforms or Website. WhatsApp and Messenger are coming soon.
  • Filters: All, Unread, Unreplied (the customer wrote last), Promo, Human (the AI is paused for your team), Queue (waiting for a department) and Failed.
  • Search: finds a chat by name, e-mail, phone or any word in the messages.
  • Tags on each chat: department, lead status (New lead or Inbox lead) and who is handling it.

06Working a chat

Reply as the team

Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line). The visitor sees your reply in their chat window within a few seconds. When you reply, the AI steps back and the chat is marked Human.

The switch at the right of the chat header turns the AI on or off for this one chat. If nobody on the team answers for a few minutes, the AI can take over again; you set how long under Settings → AI replies.

Save a lead

  1. Press Save lead. The customer's name, e-mail and phone are filled in from the chat.
  2. Add the car's VIN (and ZIP if you have it), then press Save lead. A lead needs an e-mail or a phone number so you can reach the customer.
  3. The lead appears under Leads, is e-mailed to your CRM in ADF format, and the chat's tag changes from New lead to Inbox lead.

Book a visit

  1. Press Book visit, pick the day (today or later) and time, and add the car if you like.
  2. Press Book visit. It appears under Appointments and in the chat.

Send cars, files, emoji and voice notes

  • Inventory (car icon at the left of the message box): search your stock, tick one or more cars, press Send selected. Each car arrives as a card with photo, price and a link to the car's page, just like the AI's cars.
  • Attach (paper clip): a document, photo or video, or an audio file.
  • Emoji (smiley): pick an emoji or search for one.
  • Voice note (microphone): record, press stop, then Send. Allow the microphone the first time.

Search, star and other tools

  • Search this chat (magnifier): shows only the messages with your word.
  • Star a message from its ⋯ menu, then press Starred messages (star icon) to see only those.
  • Rename (pencil next to the name): your own label for the customer; it is not sent to them.
  • Customer page: opens a page for this customer with the chat history, cars and booking, to share with them.
  • Flag as promo: pauses the AI and marks the chat for a person; Unflag turns the AI back on.
  • Activity chips under the header (Department, Contact form, visits, car inquiry, visit booked): click one to jump to that moment in the chat.

07Leads

Menu → Leads

Customers interested in a car, from the website chat and from your team. Each new lead is also e-mailed to your CRM.

  • By customer lists people; By car groups leads per vehicle with a lead count.
  • Search by name, e-mail, phone or VIN; filter by status and dates; Reset clears the filters.
  • CSV downloads the list. In each row: the eye opens the lead, the speech bubble opens its chat, and the bin deletes it after asking.

08Appointments

Menu → Appointments

Showroom visits booked from chats and from the website, with booking counts at the top. Filter by name, source and visit date (Visit from / to), export CSV, and in each row view the booking, open its chat or delete it.

09Vehicles

Menu → Vehicles

Your stock from the AutoPulse car feed. For each car you see its leads, how often it came up in chats, page views, and how many customers tapped Call, Text or WhatsApp on its page.

  • Search by make, model, body, new or used, year, price and miles.
  • View car opens photos, price, specifications and activity. Copy public link copies the car's page link to send to a customer.

10Conversations

Menu → Conversations

A log with one row per chat: when it started, the customer, the first question, message and follow-up counts, session time, clicks and whether a visit was booked. Sort and filter it, open the full transcript, or open the chat in the Inbox. CSV exports the rows and Transcripts CSV exports every message.

11Visitors

Menu → Visitors

Everyone who browses your website while visitor tracking is on: each visit, the pages they opened and the cars they looked at. Visitors who chat become Identified. The cards at the top (Visitors, Identified, Unverified, Anonymous, Came back, Looked at cars) filter the list. Export downloads the list or every page view as CSV or Excel.

Visitor tracking is off until you turn it on in Website chat → Visitor tracking.

12Website chat

Menu → Website chat (owners and managers)

Put the chat on your website

  1. Press Copy embed code and give it to whoever manages your website. It is one line that goes before </body> on every page.
  2. Press Open test page to try the chat before it is live.
  3. Set the chat's name, colours, position, welcome message and opening hours, then press Save chat widget.

Pop-up messages

Short messages that appear next to the closed chat bubble, each with up to three one-tap buttons (open the cars, ask a question, open the booking form). Add, reorder and remove messages, or press Start from our suggestions. Press Save pop-up messages; your website shows them within 5 minutes. Try it on the test page shows them as a new visitor would.

Visitor tracking

Choose Off, Notice (a short notice tells visitors) or Consent (visitors agree first), how long to keep the data, and which of your websites to track. Then press Save visitor tracking.

13Store

Menu → Store (owners and managers)

  • Store details: address (press Find position from address for the map), contact person, call and text numbers, manager alerts and time zone. The AI and your public pages use these.
  • What the AI knows: links the AI shares when asked (inventory, credit application, trade-in, website) and a short note about the dealership. Open What the AI is told to read the exact text the AI receives.
  • My stores (owners): add another store by its website. Check stock shows what the car feed has for that website first.

14Team and roles

Menu → Team (owners and managers)

  1. Press Add person. Enter name, e-mail and title, pick the role (Agent or Manager), their departments and stores.
  2. Choose Temporary password (shown once: copy it and give it to them) or E-mail invite.
  3. They sign in and choose their own password. Use Edit or More… on a row to change a person, give a new password, turn them off or remove them.

Departments and how chats are shared

Tick the departments your website chat offers (Sales, Service, Trade-in, Finance, Parts, Other). With Route new website chats on, a chat goes to someone in the department the visitor picked; if nobody is online it waits in that department's queue and the AI keeps answering.

RoleWhat they do
OwnerEverything for their stores: team, store details, website chat, pages, settings.
ManagerRuns the store and its agents: chats, leads, visits, team members, store settings.
AgentReads and answers the store's chats, saves leads and books visits.

The full table is on the Team screen under What each role can do. The app refuses anything a role is not allowed to do.

15Pages to share

Menu → Pages

  • Dealership page: your address, hours, departments, team, cars and a QR code. Choose Private link (opens only with its link) or Public, then press Save.
  • My page: each person's own page with a Chat with me button. Chats started there come straight to that person.
  • Share the links or QR codes on window stickers, business cards and e-mail signatures.

16Settings

Menu → Settings

  • My account: your name, photo, phone and address; your time zone (every date and time in the app follows it); and Change password (12 characters or more; your other devices are signed out).
  • AI replies: the master switch for AI answers in your store, a switch for the Website chat (and for each other channel once connected), and If nobody on the team replies within…, the minutes before the AI takes back a chat a person is handling (or Never).
  • E-mails & alerts: who gets an e-mail when a chat waits in a queue, a customer asks for a person, or a new lead arrives; and the ADF address your CRM imports leads and visits from.

17What visitors see

The chat on your website, from the customer's side.

  1. The bubble sits in the corner in your colours. Pop-up messages appear next to it; visitors can close them or turn them off under ⋯.
  2. The visitor picks a department, then gives name, e-mail and phone once. Names take letters only, and the form checks the e-mail and phone.
  3. The AI answers from your stock with car cards: I'm interested, View, Save, Share.
  4. Book a visit picks a date (from today on) and time. Request a call back sends a lead to your team.
  5. Talk to a person alerts your team; the button changes to Team notified. Outside opening hours the chat says when you are back.
  6. When the visitor comes back later, the chat continues where it stopped.

19Tips and answers

Can the AI and my team both answer?

Yes. The AI answers until someone on your team replies. Then the chat is yours, and the AI only steps back in if nobody answers within the time set under Settings → AI replies.

Where do my leads go?

They are listed under Leads and e-mailed in ADF format to the address under Settings → E-mails & alerts, so your CRM can import them.

How do I see a customer's whole story?

Open the chat: the chips under the header show the department, contact form, visits and booked visit. Leads, Appointments and Conversations each link back to the chat.

Something looks wrong?

Press Help on that screen for an explanation, or contact AutoPulse at info@autopulse.ai.
